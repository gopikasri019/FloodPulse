# 🌊 FloodPulse

## Urban Flood Intelligence & Emergency Response Platform

FloodPulse is an AI-powered urban flood intelligence platform designed for real-time flood monitoring, risk prediction, emergency response, and coordinated disaster management.

It combines flood-risk analysis, hydrological monitoring, drainage intelligence, AI-assisted recommendations, and role-based emergency coordination in a single platform.

## 🚀 Live Deployment

**Live Website:**

## 🎯 Key Features

- 🌧️ Real-time flood risk monitoring
- 🗺️ Multi-city and location-based flood intelligence
- 💧 Drainage and water-level monitoring
- 🤖 AI-assisted flood risk analysis and recommendations
- 🚨 Emergency alerts and citizen SOS
- 🏛️ Government official dashboard
- 🌊 Dam operator dashboard
- 👥 Volunteer coordination
- 🏠 Citizen emergency support
- 📊 Flood prediction and risk visualization
- 🔄 Simulation and scenario management

## 👥 Role-Based Access

### Government Officials
Monitor flood conditions, risk zones, emergency requests, and response operations.

### Dam Operators
Monitor reservoir conditions, simulate releases, and evaluate downstream flood risks.

### Citizens
View flood risks, receive alerts, and submit emergency SOS requests.

### Volunteers
View emergency requirements and coordinate assistance in affected areas.
